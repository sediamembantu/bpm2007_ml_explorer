# MALAYSIA AIRPORTS HOLDINGS BERHAD - ANNUAL REPORT 2024
# NARRATIVE SHAREHOLDER DISCLOSURE

## SHAREHOLDER STRUCTURE

Malaysia Airports Holdings Berhad, being a strategic infrastructure company, has a diverse shareholder base comprising primarily government entities and government-linked investment companies. As at 31 December 2024, the company's issued and paid-up capital of 2,500,000,000 ordinary shares of RM1.00 each is distributed among several key shareholders.

The largest shareholder is the Ministry of Finance Malaysia, which holds a controlling interest of 53.45% of the issued share capital. This substantial ownership reflects the strategic importance of Malaysia Airports Holdings Berhad to the nation's aviation infrastructure development.

Khazanah Nasional Berhad, the sovereign wealth fund of Malaysia, maintains a significant stake of 25.80% in the company. This investment is part of Khazanah's strategic portfolio management and aligns with its mandate to develop Malaysia's key infrastructure sectors.

The Employees Provident Fund, Malaysia's largest pension fund, holds a meaningful position of 12.30% in Malaysia Airports Holdings Berhad. The EPF's investment represents a long-term commitment to the aviation sector and supports the fund's objective of generating stable returns for its members.

Kumpulan Wang Simpanan Pekerja, the pension fund for civil servants, holds 5.20% of the company. KWAP's investment in Malaysia Airports Holdings Berhad is part of its diversified portfolio strategy focusing on blue-chip companies with stable cash flows.

Permodalan Nasional Berhad, the country's largest investment management company, maintains a stake of 1.85% in the company. PNB's investment is managed through its unit trust funds and represents confidence in the long-term prospects of Malaysia's aviation industry.

Lembaga Tabung Haji, the pilgrimage fund, holds 1.20% of Malaysia Airports Holdings Berhad. Tabung Haji's investment is part of its Shariah-compliant investment strategy focusing on companies with strong ethical governance.

Foreign ownership in Malaysia Airports Holdings Berhad remains limited at 0.20%. The foreign shareholders include Singapore Changi Airport Fund, which holds 0.15%, and Dubai Airports Investment LLC, which holds 0.05%.

The ownership structure of Malaysia Airports Holdings Berhad demonstrates strong government support, with total government and government-linked entities holding 99.80% of the company's shares. This high level of government ownership provides stability and enables the company to undertake long-term infrastructure development projects.

## SUBSTANTIAL SHAREHOLDERS

Substantial shareholders, defined as those holding more than 5% of the issued share capital, include the Ministry of Finance Malaysia with 53.45%, Khazanah Nasional Berhad with 25.80%, and the Employees Provident Fund with 12.30%. These three shareholders collectively hold 91.55% of the company, providing them with significant control over corporate decisions.

Kumpulan Wang Simpanan Pekerja, holding 5.20%, is also classified as a substantial shareholder. KWAP's investment provides it with a meaningful stake in the company's strategic direction.

## CHANGES IN SHAREHOLDINGS

During the financial year ended 31 December 2024, there were several significant changes in shareholdings. The Ministry of Finance Malaysia increased its stake from 52.80% to 53.45%, representing an acquisition of 16.25 million shares. This increase was part of the government's strategic initiative to strengthen its control over critical infrastructure.

Khazanah Nasional Berhad reduced its holding from 26.50% to 25.80%, disposing of 17.5 million shares. The divestment was part of Khazanah's portfolio rebalancing exercise and aimed to optimize its investment allocation.

The Employees Provident Fund increased its stake from 11.80% to 12.30%, purchasing 12.5 million shares. This increase reflects EPF's confidence in the long-term growth prospects of Malaysia's aviation sector.

Kumpulan Wang Simpanan Pekerja maintained its position at 5.20%, with no change in its shareholding during the year. KWAP continues to view Malaysia Airports Holdings Berhad as a core holding in its portfolio.

## OWNERSHIP CLASSIFICATION

Based on the ownership structure, Malaysia Airports Holdings Berhad can be classified as a government-controlled company. The Ministry of Finance Malaysia, holding 53.45%, exercises effective control over the company. Combined with Khazanah Nasional Berhad's 25.80% and EPF's 12.30%, government entities collectively hold 91.55% of the company.

The company is also considered a government-linked company (GLC) due to Khazanah Nasional Berhad's significant stake. Khazanah's 25.80% ownership position provides it with substantial influence over the company's strategic direction while allowing for operational autonomy.

## FOREIGN OWNERSHIP

Foreign ownership in Malaysia Airports Holdings Berhad is minimal at 0.20%. Singapore Changi Airport Fund holds 0.15% of the company, representing a strategic investment by Singapore's airport operator. Dubai Airports Investment LLC holds 0.05%, reflecting the growing interest from Middle Eastern airport operators in Malaysia's aviation sector.

The limited foreign ownership is consistent with the government's policy of maintaining control over strategic infrastructure assets. The company's governance structure ensures that foreign investors participate as minority shareholders without affecting national control.

## SHAREHOLDER RIGHTS AND PROTECTIONS

All shareholders, regardless of their shareholding percentage, enjoy equal rights under the company's Articles of Association. Shareholders are entitled to attend annual general meetings, vote on resolutions, receive dividends, and access company information.

The government, as the controlling shareholder, exercises its rights through the Ministry of Finance Malaysia. The ministry's representatives on the board of directors ensure that the company's activities align with national interests while maintaining commercial viability.

Minority shareholders, including foreign investors, are protected by Malaysia's corporate governance framework. The company adheres to the Malaysian Code on Corporate Governance and maintains transparent disclosure practices.

## BENEFICIAL OWNERSHIP

The beneficial owners of the Ministry of Finance Malaysia's 53.45% stake is the Government of Malaysia. This ownership is held directly on behalf of the Malaysian public and is managed by the Treasury.

Khazanah Nasional Berhad's 25.80% stake is beneficially owned by the Government of Malaysia through the Ministry of Finance. Khazanah acts as the investment vehicle for managing Malaysia's strategic investments.

The Employees Provident Fund's 12.30% stake is beneficially owned by EPF's 15 million members. The EPF Board manages these investments on behalf of the contributors to generate returns for their retirement savings.

Kumpulan Wang Simpanan Pekerja's 5.20% stake is beneficially owned by Malaysia's civil servants and pensioners. KWAP manages these investments to fund the pension obligations of the public sector.

## CROSS-HOLDINGS AND RELATED ENTITIES

Malaysia Airports Holdings Berhad does not have any significant cross-holdings in its major shareholders. The ownership structure is straightforward, with each shareholder holding its stake directly.

However, there are indirect relationships among shareholders through common government ownership. The Ministry of Finance Malaysia, Khazanah Nasional Berhad, EPF, KWAP, PNB, and Tabung Haji are all ultimately owned or controlled by the Malaysian Government.

The company maintains subsidiaries including KL International Airport Berhad, Malaysia Airports (Sepang) Sdn Bhd, and Malaysia Airports (MAHB) Technologies Sdn Bhd, which are 100% owned by Malaysia Airports Holdings Berhad.

## RELATED PARTY TRANSACTIONS

Malaysia Airports Holdings Berhad has related party transactions with its substantial shareholders. The Ministry of Finance Malaysia, as the controlling shareholder, enters into regulatory agreements and concession contracts with the company. These transactions are conducted at arm's length and are approved by the board of directors.

Khazanah Nasional Berhad provides strategic advisory services to the company on regional expansion and investment opportunities. These services are remunerated at market rates and are subject to board approval.

The Employees Provident Fund has investments in the company's subsidiary bond issuances. These investments are part of EPF's fixed income portfolio and are structured as commercially viable transactions.

The shareholder distribution of Malaysia Airports Holdings Berhad as at 31 December 2024 can be summarized as follows:

- Government ownership (Ministry of Finance Malaysia, Khazanah Nasional Berhad, EPF, KWAP, PNB, Tabung Haji): 99.80%
- Foreign ownership (Singapore Changi Airport Fund, Dubai Airports Investment LLC): 0.20%
- Total ownership analyzed: 100.00%

The ownership structure reflects the strategic importance of Malaysia Airports Holdings Berhad to the nation's aviation infrastructure development and the government's commitment to maintaining control over critical assets.
