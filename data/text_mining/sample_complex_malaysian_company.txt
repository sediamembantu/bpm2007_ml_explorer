## 2. SUBSTANTIAL SHAREHOLDERS (5% and above)

The following substantial shareholders hold more than 5% of the issued share capital:

### Employees Provident Fund (EPF) - 35.25%

The Employees Provident Fund holds 35.25% of the issued share capital through direct and indirect interests.
- Direct interest: 28.50%
- Indirect interest through unit trusts: 6.75%

**Beneficial Owner:** Malaysian Government (through Ministry of Finance)

### Kumpulan Wang Simpanan Pekerja (KWAP) - 12.50%

KWAP holds 12.50% of the issued share capital as a direct investment on behalf of the Malaysian Government.

**Type:** Direct Investment
**Beneficial Owner:** Malaysian Government

### Khazanah Nasional Berhad - 10.00%

Khazanah Nasional Berhad holds 10.00% as a strategic sovereign investment.

**Type:** Direct Investment
**Ownership Classification:** Government-Linked Investment Company (GLIC)

- Direct interest: 8.75%

**Type:** Direct Investment
**Ultimate Parent:** Kuwait Finance House (KFH), Kuwait
**Classification:** Foreign Direct Investment (FDI)
